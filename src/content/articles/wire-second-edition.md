**AWIEF: under a month to go.** Nominations for the 2026 AWIEF Awards for African women founders close on 3 August at 23:59 CAT. Self-nomination is allowed and costs nothing. If you qualified when we flagged it last week and have not entered, this is the reminder.

**FirstRand's small business war chest grew by US$310 million.** The African Development Bank approved a US$310 million package for FirstRand to scale lending to MSMEs, women entrepreneurs and agribusinesses, stacking on top of the IFC's US$100 million MSME facility. Translation: FNB business banking has fresh institutional pressure to approve qualifying loans. Women-owned and agri businesses should apply saying so.

**Absa-HEINEKEN R1,2 billion drive is live.** The November 2025 launch (R600 million HEINEKEN ESD funding administered by Absa plus R600 million Absa co-lending) targets 100-plus black-owned SMEs. Suppliers and potential suppliers to the beverage value chain should ask Absa business banking about qualification.

**SANRAL put R1,4 billion into Eastern Cape roads since April.** Multiple upgrade and maintenance contracts were awarded across the province. Local SMMEs near the sites: main contractors will be building subcontractor and supplier lists now, for work from aggregates to catering.

**The R9,5 billion caution.** SANRAL's biggest maintenance award of the quarter is under legal challenge. If your pipeline assumed subcontracting from it, build in delay: contested awards can freeze for months.

**Tholoana watch continues.** The SAB Foundation's 18-month enterprise programme takes up to 60 businesses a year; the last intake closed 8 September 2025. Historically the call opens around this time of year. Check sabfoundation.co.za weekly if you are 51 percent black-owned and trading 12+ months.

**Anzisha opens in spring.** Africa's award for entrepreneurs aged 15 to 22 has opened its last two intakes around October, closing in November. Young founders: use these months to get your trading records clean, because the prize rewards documented growth.

**Stat of the week: R1,07 trillion.** The total public infrastructure spend planned over the next three years, of which transport takes R417,6 billion, the largest share of any sector. Roads, rail and ports are where the tender pipeline will be thickest through 2029.

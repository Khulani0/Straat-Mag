**AWIEF Awards close 3 August.** Nominations for the 2026 AWIEF Awards, the continental honours for African women founders, close on 3 August 2026 at 23:59 CAT. Categories include Woman to Watch, Technology/Innovation, Creative Visionary and Impact for Good. Self-nomination is allowed. Winners are announced 11 November at the AWIEF Conference at the CTICC in Cape Town.

**Pitch n Grow reportedly closes 20 July.** AWIEF's pan-African pitch competition for women building technology-driven ventures is listed as closing 20 July 2026. Confirm the date on AWIEF's official channels before building your week around it; listings sites have been the primary source.

**A new R300 million youth fund exists.** The Peo Pele Youth Fund, launched in QwaQwa during Youth Month by Small Business Development Minister Stella Tembisa Ndabeni, targets youth-owned businesses with stage-based support from start-up to growth. It runs through SEDFA and builds on the Youth Challenge Fund. Application details are flowing through SEDFA channels; The Wire will track them.

**Township funding ceiling tripled.** The 2026/27 budget raised the Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme's per-business ceiling from R1 million to R3 million and set aside R710 million. If TREP previously felt too small for your equipment list, re-look.

**Student founders: the InnoVarsity is live.** The EDHE Absa Entrepreneurship InnoVarsity 2026 (the rebranded Intervarsity) opened entries on 16 April, with up to R1,2 million in prize money across categories. Entry runs through the EDHE coordinator on your campus; closing dates are set per university, so ask this week, not next month.

**Procurement rulebook: comments are in, watch for the final text.** The comment window on National Treasury's draft General Public Procurement Regulations closed on 15 June 2026. The draft proposes set-asides reserving sub-R20 million contracts for categories including youth, women and small enterprises. Finalisation puts the Public Procurement Act 2024 into real effect; The Ledger will break down the final regulations when gazetted.

**Tholoana watch.** The SAB Foundation's Tholoana Enterprise Programme (18 months of business support for majority black-owned businesses trading at least a year) historically opens applications around mid-year; the last intake closed 8 September 2025. Check sabfoundation.co.za monthly if you qualify.

**Stat of the week: R137,3 million.** The 2026 allocation to the Technology Innovation Agency's seed fund and commercialisation hubs, part of a R1,2 billion boost to the agency. If you are sitting on a prototype-stage technology, the state's appetite just grew.

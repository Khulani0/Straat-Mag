## Why the CSD comes first

Nothing in government procurement happens until you are on the Central Supplier Database. Since 1 April 2016 it has been the single supplier record for national, provincial and municipal government. Registration is free at csd.gov.za.

## What to have ready

Have three things in order before you start: your CIPC company registration, your SARS tax compliance status pin, and bank details your bank can confirm. The database verifies directors against Home Affairs records, so your details must match exactly.

## The number that matters

Registration costs nothing. Anyone who asks you to pay to get onto the CSD, or to "fast-track" it, is running a scam. Once registered you receive an MAAA supplier number that every bid document will ask for.

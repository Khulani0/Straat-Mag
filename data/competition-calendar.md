## Structural note (verified, important)

SEFA, SEDA and the Co-operative Banks Development Agency merged into a single agency, the Small Enterprise Development and Finance Agency (SEDFA), with effect from 1 October 2024 under the National Small Enterprise Amendment Act of 2024. Applications for former SEFA loan products and former SEDA business support now run through SEDFA. Any article that describes SEFA or SEDA as standalone agencies is out of date.

## ROLLING PROGRAMMES (no fixed deadline, always open unless noted)

### SEDFA loan finance (former SEFA products) | VERIFIED

- What: Government development finance for SMMEs and co-operatives: direct loans, bridging finance, asset finance, wholesale lending via intermediaries. Reported range roughly R50 000 to R15 million.
- Who: SA-registered small enterprises and co-operatives. Applications via SEDFA (portal and branches).
- Source: https://www.sedfa.org.za/ (accessed 4 July 2026)
- Source: https://www.dsbd.gov.za/sites/default/files/2025-04/SEDFA%20-%20Annual%20Performance%20Plan%20for%202025-26%20(Revised)_010425.pdf (accessed 4 July 2026)

### NYDA Grant Programme | VERIFIED (re-check exact tiers against official docs before each article)

- What: Non-repayable grants plus compulsory business development support for young entrepreneurs. Third-party guides report tiers from R1 000 (survivalist) up to R250 000 for co-operatives and agriculture/technology projects; commonly cited individual ceiling R200 000.
- Who: SA citizens aged 18 to 35, business 100 percent youth-owned.
- Source (official): https://www.nyda.gov.za/Portals/0/WebSitesCreative_MyContentManager/1092/NYDA_Grant_Programme.pdf (accessed 4 July 2026)
- Source: https://www.vukuzenzele.gov.za/guide-get-nyda-funding (accessed 4 July 2026)

### Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme (TREP) | VERIFIED

- What: DSBD flagship for township and rural businesses: blended funding, working capital, equipment. The 2026/27 budget raised the funding ceiling from R1 million to R3 million and set aside R710 million.
- Who: Township and rural entrepreneurs; applications through SEDFA (and previously the SEDA/SEFA/NEF/NYDA common template).
- Source: http://www.dsbd.gov.za/programme/township-and-rural-entrepreneurship-programme (accessed 4 July 2026)
- Source: https://www.thesouthafrican.com/business/township-and-rural-entrepreneurship-programme/ (accessed 4 July 2026)

### Peo Pele Youth Fund | VERIFIED (new, launched June 2026)

- What: New R300 million fund for youth-owned businesses, structured by business stage from start-up to growth. Builds on the Youth Challenge Fund. Implemented through SEDFA.
- Who: Youth-owned businesses (DSBD youth definition, 18 to 35).
- Launched: Youth Month 2026, QwaQwa, by Small Business Development Minister Stella Tembisa Ndabeni.
- Source: https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-stella-tembisa-ndabeni-youth-day-commemoration-and-launch-peo-pele-youth (accessed 4 July 2026)
- Source: https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/government-launches-peo-pele-youth-fund-to-back-young-entrepreneurs/ (accessed 4 July 2026)

### IDC Gro-E Youth Scheme | VERIFIED

- What: IDC funding for youth-owned enterprises, R1 million to R50 million per project, loans at prime less 3 percent for businesses more than 50 percent youth-owned. Job creation requirement: maximum R500 000 cost per job.
- Who: South Africans and permanent residents up to age 35, youth shareholding above 50 percent, operational involvement required.
- Source: https://www.idc.co.za/gro-e-youth-scheme/ (accessed 4 July 2026)

### TIA Seed Fund | VERIFIED

- What: Technology Innovation Agency seed funding to move research outputs and tech ideas to prototype and commercialisation. R137,3 million allocated to the seed fund and commercialisation hubs in 2026.
- Who: Innovators at universities and science councils (via tech transfer offices) and tech SMMEs (via TIA and regional partners).
- Source: https://www.tia.org.za/seed-fund/ (accessed 4 July 2026)
- Source: https://techcentral.co.za/big-win-for-south-african-innovation-agency/282473/ (accessed 4 July 2026)

### Old Mutual Masisizane Fund | VERIFIED

- What: Development finance from R250 000 to R20 million plus business support. Bias to women, youth and people with disabilities in rural, peri-urban and township areas. Requires comprehensive insurance including SASRIA cover before funds transfer.
- Who: Juristic persons (company, CC, co-operative) at least 51 percent black-owned, SARS compliant, a shareholder full-time in the business.
- Source: https://www.oldmutual.co.za/business/solutions/masisizane/ (accessed 4 July 2026)

### FNB Vumela Enterprise Development Fund | VERIFIED

- What: Equity and venture debt for "missing middle" high-growth SMEs, managed by FNB with Edge Growth. Vumela 4 committed R200 million. First three phases deployed R404 million and created over 6 500 jobs (2010 to 2021).
- Who: High-growth SMEs that fall between microfinance and private equity.
- Source: https://www.fnb.co.za/business-banking/specialised-finance/vumela.html (accessed 4 July 2026)
- Source: https://vumelafund.com/ (accessed 4 July 2026)

### Youth Challenge Fund (DSBD) | VERIFIED (status: being superseded)

- What: Youth start-up support programme launched 2021. To date: 4 828 applications, 121 enterprises approved for R362,5 million, R224,6 million disbursed to 83 enterprises. The new Peo Pele Youth Fund explicitly builds on it; treat YCF as winding into Peo Pele and verify current intake status before citing it as open.
- Source: http://www.dsbd.gov.za/programme/youth-challenge-fund (accessed 4 July 2026)
